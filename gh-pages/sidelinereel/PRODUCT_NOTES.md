# Priority hypothesis (draft)
**Build next: make the per-kid reel + weekly family recap reliably land and get watched** — i.e. highlight-detection accuracy/coverage for every rostered kid (no kid with zero clips) plus recap delivery & open tracking.

Why:
- It is the core job: families come for *their* kid's moments; coaches stay if uploading turns into recaps with zero effort.
- Evidence over loudness: weigh usage data (reel opens per family, % kids with ≥1 clip, coach upload→recap completion, support tickets on missed kids) over the loudest stakeholder/club ask.
- Vanity metrics caution: total uploads or clips generated ≠ value; track weekly families-watching-reel and coach re-upload retention.
- Defer: social/sharing feeds, advanced editing, monetization add-ons until the core loop retains.

Success metric: % of rostered families who open their kid's reel within 48h of the weekly recap; guardrail: % kids with zero clips.
Risks: privacy/consent for minors (opt-in, family-only links) — must ship alongside.
